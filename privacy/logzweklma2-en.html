1Who we are and scope

Riddle & Word - Word Link (لغز وكلمة - وصلة كلمات) is developed by OFQ Ventures (developer account OFQ Labs on Google Play). This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app. It covers this app only, not third-party sites or services.

2Data the app handles

The app does not require an account and never asks for personal information.

Data typeStatusDetails
Name, email, phone numberNot collectedNo sign-in and no forms.

Advertising ID, IP address, ad interaction dataVia Google AdMobTo serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See the next section.

3Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which keeps it free. Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID and general technical data to serve relevant ads and measure their performance.

  • Where required (e.g. EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown before any personalised ad.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → "Ad privacy options".
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from partner apps.

4Your rights and choices

  • Opt out of personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Email us with any data request and we will respond within 30 days.

5Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will act promptly.

6Security and retention

We do not operate servers that store user data, so we retain nothing about you. Local data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The last-updated date appears at the top, and material changes will be noted in the app.

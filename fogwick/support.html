Support (English)

Need help or found a bug? Email contact@ofqventures.com with your device model and the level number.

  • Lost progress? The game plays offline and saves progress on your device only; deleting the app or changing phones starts a new game.
  • Out of energy? Energy refills by itself every minute, there is a daily gift, and an optional short video gives extra energy.
  • Ads. See the privacy policy for when ads show and how to change your ad choices.
  • Delete my data: clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app (details).

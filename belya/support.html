Support (English)

Need help or found a bug? Email contact@ofqventures.com.

  • Lost progress? Belya plays offline and saves progress on your device only; deleting the app or changing phones starts a new game.
  • Out of coins? Every level you clear for the first time pays bonus coins, there is a daily gift, and you can double a level's coins with a short video.
  • Delete my data: clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app (details).

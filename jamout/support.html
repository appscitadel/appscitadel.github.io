JAM OUT support

Slide every coloured block out through the gate of its colour until the board is empty. Fewer moves earn more stars.

Common questions

  • I'm stuck. Use Hint: it lights up the right block and shows where to push one aside. Undo and Restart are always free; Smash breaks one block.
  • Out of hints or hammers? Spend coins, or watch an optional rewarded ad.
  • Colours are hard to tell apart. Turn on Colour-blind mode in Settings — gates and blocks get symbols.
  • Progress lost after reinstalling. Progress is stored only on your device; uninstalling or clearing app data removes it.
  • Ads. See the privacy policy for when ads show and how to change your ad choices.

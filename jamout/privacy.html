Privacy policy

Publisher: OFQ Labs (OFQ Ventures). App: JAM OUT (com.ofqventures.jamout). Last updated: 2026-11-08.

JAM OUT is a block-sliding puzzle game (slide each coloured block out through the gate of its colour) that runs fully offline. There is no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your data.

What is collected

  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; iOS IDFA if you allow it): the Google AdMob SDK in the app, and Unity Ads when it serves an ad through AdMob, collect it along with device information and approximate location from the IP address to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or turn it off in your device settings.

Ads

Ad partners: Google AdMob (primary) and Unity Ads (bidding through AdMob mediation). Rewarded ads are always optional. Full-screen ads appear only after you win a level, after your first 10 levels, at most once every 3 levels and at least 2 minutes apart. A small banner ad is shown at the bottom of the screen. Where consent is required (EEA/UK) we ask first, and without your consent we request non-personalized ads only. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data. On iPhone we ask before using the IDFA. Where it applies, you can change your choices in Settings → "Ad choices". Both partners process data under Google's policy and Unity's privacy policy. There are no in-app purchases, and we collect no payment data.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device: clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you to delete. Questions: contact@ofqventures.com.

Children

Family-friendly; no chat or player-to-player contact; not directed at children under 13.
